# Geneva, Summer or Snow

A family trip plan for Geneva, Switzerland with a small child, in two versions: a July summer week, or an early-December week with a beginner ski day.

**Live site:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/geneva/
**Map:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/geneva/map/

## What's on the site

- A week-long itinerary for each season (switch with the Summer / Winter toggle)
- A CERN Science Gateway visit
- A beginner ski day at Villars-Gryon
- A day trip to the Montreux Christmas market
- Hotels and restaurants, with Tripadvisor ratings
- Advice on travelling with a small child
- Flights, rough costs, and cited sources for everything
- Interactive map of hotels, sights, food and day trips

## Notes

- The site is static HTML served by GitHub Pages; there is no build step in this repo.
- Pages are marked `noindex`, so search engines are asked not to list them.
- Photo credits and every research source are listed in the Sources section.
- Music: *Einzug der Greifler in Bulle*, Swiss folk music played by the Mosibuebä. CC BY-SA 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons.
