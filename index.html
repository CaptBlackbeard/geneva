Geneva

Summer on the lake · or snow in December · with a day at CERN

A week, two ways

One city, two very different trips. In July, Geneva is a lake town: swimming off the jetties, little boats across the harbour that cost nothing with the hotel's transport card, and light until half past nine. In December it is cheese and chocolate and Christmas lights, dark by five, with the Alps close enough for one day of learning to ski.

Both weeks include a day at CERN. Both are planned around a kid five and under, which mostly means parks, boats, one big thing a day, and lunch as the main meal.

Summer · July

The week by the lake

Highs around 27°C, about fifteen and a half hours of daylight, and the busiest month of the year — which mostly shows on the waterfront.

01

Out of LAX, overnight

There is no nonstop to Geneva. The quickest one-stop in our search was SWISS through Zurich at about thirteen hours door to gate; Lufthansa through Munich or Frankfurt is close behind.

02

Land, seven minutes, done

The airport train reaches Cornavin in seven minutes, and the free Geneva Transport Card — emailed by the hotel about three days before we arrive — covers it. Check in, walk to the water, watch the Jet d'Eau, find the flower clock. Early dinner, early night.

With a small one: the yellow Mouettes water taxis cross the harbour in minutes, free with the card. Most toddlers consider this the whole holiday.

03

A lake day

Morning at Parc La Grange: a playground with sand and a paddling pool, the rose garden, and Baby-Plage next door — a free little beach with showers and toilets. Back for a nap. Evening at the Bains des Pâquis, the old bathing jetty, for dinner at the buvette.

With a small one: the Pâquis is deep water off pebbles, not a paddling spot. Baby-Plage and the park pools are the toddler swims.

04

CERN

Tram 18 to Meyrin, CERN. Science Gateway opens at nine, is free, and is closed on Mondays — so this is any day but a Monday. Two hours in the exhibitions, lunch at the Big Bang Café or a picnic on the lawn, back by mid-afternoon. Full details in the CERN section below.

With a small one: nothing there is programmed for under-fours, but the halls are hands-on and pushchair-friendly. Treat it as a wander, not a tour.

05

The lake by paddle steamer

CGN still runs Belle Époque paddle steamers out of Geneva, and a family pass is planned for summer 2027. Under-sixes ride free. Or take the train an hour up the lake to Lavaux, the UNESCO vineyard terraces, and come back along the water.

With a small one: you can watch the paddle wheels and the engine room working. Budget time for staring.

06

Old town and Carouge

Up through the old town to St Pierre — the towers are 157 steps and CHF 7 for adults — then down to Parc des Bastions for the playground and the giant chess boards. Fondue lunch at Les Armures. In the afternoon, tram to Carouge, the Italianate town next door. On Thursday to Sunday evenings in July, the free open-air cinema by the lake.

With a small one: old-town cobbles and the Bastions stairs are hard work with a pushchair. Bring the carrier for this one.

07

Home

Seven minutes to the airport. The station shops open even on Sundays, for the last chocolate.

Early winter · December

The week in the cold

Highs around 6°C, under nine hours of daylight and about forty-nine hours of sun in the whole month. Cosy, quiet, and close to the snow.

When in December. The first half is the sweet spot: before the French school holidays (from 18 December 2027), before CERN's Christmas closures (last winter: no tours from 23 December to 5 January), and while the Christmas market is on. The catch is that early December is also when ski snow is least reliable. We picked mid-December and kept the ski day movable. Geneva school holidays

01

Out of LAX, overnight

Same routes as summer. December 2027 flights were not on sale yet when we checked — see Getting there.

02

Land, and go straight to the market

Seven minutes into town on the free transport card. Then Noël au Quai, the Christmas market on the lakefront: last year it ran from 19 November to 24 December, with a children's village, an ice rink and a Ferris wheel. The Jet d'Eau runs 10:00 to 16:00 in December, if the wind allows.

With a small one: the bise, the north-east wind down the lake, is the cold you actually feel. Hats that tie on.

03

Montreux, and a flying Santa

Just over an hour up the lake on a direct train, and the market starts two hundred metres from the station: more than 150 lit chalets along the quays, the Belle Époque covered market, and a Ferris wheel on Place du Marché. Last year it ran from 20 November to 24 December. Montreux Noël

After lunch, bus 201 to Château de Chillon: fourteen minutes to a real lakeside castle with dungeons and towers. Then back to the quays before five, because every evening Santa flies over the market — at 17:00, 18:00 and 19:00, and at 16:00 too at weekends. Château de Chillon

With a small one: the flying Santa is free, and the organisers say the best spot is on the quays near the Freddie Mercury statue. Go on a Monday, Tuesday or Thursday if you can — their advice for a quieter visit. Pushchairs are fine on the flat quays but stay in the cloakroom at Chillon, so bring the carrier. Skip Santa's mountain house at Rochers-de-Naye: the organisers say it is too high for under-threes.

04

Escalade weekend, in the old town

Up to St Pierre and Maison Tavel, the old town's oldest house, which is pay-what-you-wish, warm inside, and has a huge model of Geneva in 1850 to peer at. Fondue lunch at Les Armures, round the corner — there is a full suit of armour in the dining room. A chocolate stop on the way down: most shops close on Sundays.

Every December Geneva celebrates the night of 11 to 12 December 1602, when the Duke of Savoy's troops tried to climb the city walls and were thrown back. Costumed processions with torches through the old town, mulled wine, and chocolate cauldrons — marmites — in every chocolate shop window. Last year it ran 11 to 13 December. The 2027 dates are not out yet; the weekend of 10 to 12 December is our guess. Geneva Tourism

With a small one: a chocolate cauldron is an excellent bribe for the walk back down the hill.

05

CERN

Warm, free and indoors, which in December counts double. It is open on Sundays, when it runs the most shows and workshops, and closed on Mondays. Tram 18 from Cornavin.

With a small one: free lockers for the coats, lifts everywhere, baby-changing.

06

The ski day

The 07:05 train to Aigle, the bus up to Villars-Gryon, a family lesson in the village by ten. Practise on the free conveyor slopes, or take the cog railway up to Bretaye if the village snow is thin. Sledge if skiing is not happening. Back in Geneva for dinner. Everything is in the ski section below.

With a small one: the ski school takes children from three. Short, with breaks, and a sledge as the backup plan.

07

Home

Seven minutes to the airport, a box of marmites in the hand luggage.

Both seasons

A day at CERN

The home of the Large Hadron Collider is twenty-five minutes from the old town by tram, it costs nothing, and its new science centre was built for curious people of every size.

CERN Science Gateway opened in October 2023: a Renzo Piano building of glass tubes and a solar-panel canopy, paid for entirely by donations. Inside are three exhibitions — Discover CERN, Our Universe and Quantum World — plus labs, shows and a café.

One honest thing before you go: you will not see the collider itself. The LHC is a 27 km ring a hundred metres underground, and CERN's own visitor FAQ says its tunnel is never open to visitors. It is also switched off right now: the last beams ran on 27 June 2026 and the machine is in a planned upgrade until around mid-2030, which covers both of our trip windows. What you do get is the Discover CERN exhibition — a full-scale recreation of the tunnel and a virtual-reality walk through it. CERN on the shutdown · visitor FAQ

Free, but register

Exhibitions, shows, labs and tours are all free. You still register, and booking online up to a month ahead guarantees entry.

Pushchair-friendly throughout, with lifts, baby-changing and 321 free lockers. No large bags. The exhibitions are officially rated 8+ and tours 12–14+, so nothing is programmed for under-fours — but the halls welcome visitors of all ages, so plan a hands-on wander rather than a tour.

The Big Bang Café does sandwiches and salads all day and burgers from 11:00 to 15:00, roughly CHF 15–30. Or picnic on the lawn beside it.

Allow one and a half to three hours. Guided tours and labs can only be booked on site that day — tours open two hours before they start — and only about one visitor in ten gets a place.

Early winter only

Learning to ski in a day

None of us ski, and that is the point of doing it this way: one good day on snow with a teacher, from a hotel we never have to leave. Not a ski holiday.

Where: Villars-Gryon, in the Vaud Alps at the far end of the lake. It was the only beginner-friendly resort of the three we compared that was due to open in early December, it needs no border crossing, and the train does it: Geneva 07:05, change at Aigle for bus 144, Villars at 08:58. Back at 16:34, in Geneva for 18:35. Under-sixes ride free.

The lesson

A private lesson with the Swiss Ski School in Villars is CHF 80 an hour plus CHF 10 for each extra person, and they take children from age three. So the whole family, together, is about CHF 100 an hour. Ask for it in the village.

ESS Villars

Practising for free

There are free conveyor-lift practice areas in the village and at Gryon, so between lessons nobody needs a lift pass. Last season children born in 2021 or later skied free.

skiresort.com

The small one

The ski school's snow garden takes ages three to six, at most six children per group for three- and four-year-olds, helmets compulsory, with childcare before and after. But it is only sold in packs of three half-days (CHF 185) with five days' notice. For a single day, the family private lesson is the realistic choice.

Jardin des Neiges

If skiing isn't working

Sledge instead. The Col de Soud run is about 1.6 km through the woods back to the village, described as suitable for children with supervision. The cog railway day pass is CHF 36, or CHF 18 for a child. There is also a free sledging slope with its own conveyor.

Villars tourism

The snow risk

Villars village sits at about 1,300 m, which is low for December; Bretaye, at about 1,800 m, is the safer snow. A 2022 study of sixteen Savoie resorts found snowmaking has not offset falling reliability at lower resorts. Keep the ski day movable.

The Cryosphere, 2022

Gear

Skis, boots and helmets rent in the village. We could not find a one-day rental price for Villars, so budget for it separately rather than trust a guess.

With a small one in Switzerland

What parents who have done it say, checked against the official sources. Each card names where it came from.

Geneva Transport Card

Anyone staying in approved accommodation registered with Geneva Tourism gets a free digital Geneva Transport Card, emailed by the host ~3 days before arrival, valid for the whole stay on unireso zone 10 (canton of Geneva except Céligny): tpg buses/trams, CFF trains and Mouettes boats, including the Cornavin-airport train. It does NOT cover attractions or the Salève cable car (in France). geneve.com calls it personal/non-transferable; tpg says it is issued in the booking holder's name and several travelling together can share one card - ask your hotel.

geneve.com · tpg.ch

Under-6s ride free

Children under 6 travel free on SBB trains, accompanied or not, and on tpg trams/buses when with a ticket-holding adult; CGN lake boats are also free under 6. At 2y9m (July 2027) and 3y2m (Dec 2027) the child needs no ticket anywhere on Swiss public transport - the Junior Travelcard / Children's Co-Travel card (CHF 30/yr each, ages 6-15) and the free Swiss Family Card are irrelevant until age 6.

sbb.ch · tpg.ch

Sunday closures and shop hours

Geneva law (LHOM) makes shops close on Sundays and public holidays; normal closing is 19:00 Mon-Thu, 19:30 Friday, 18:00 Saturday, with one late evening per week to 21:00. Stock up on nappies, milk and snacks on Saturday. On Sundays use Cornavin and airport station shops (open 365 days), petrol-station shops (Migrolino/Pronto), bakeries (may open Sat/Sun to 19:00) and family-run shops.

silgeneve.ch · switzerlanding.com

Nappies, formula, pharmacies

Migros and Coop supermarkets and pharmacies stock major nappy and formula brands, and Swiss tap water is safe for preparing formula. Most pharmacies close on Sundays; Pharma24 (Boulevard de la Cluse 38, near HUG hospital, +41 22 808 00 18) is open 24/7. Emergency medical number: 144.

tour-switzerland.ch · hug.ch

Tap and fountain water

Geneva tap water is supplied and continuously tested by SIG and meets Swiss food-safety standards; the city's public fountains deliver the same drinkable water. Use the GE-Soif map to find the nearest fountain - bring a refillable bottle. (A Sept 2024 main burst briefly made water unsafe in one area; that was resolved 1 Oct 2024.)

geneve.com · ge-soif.ch

Lake swimming for toddlers

Baby-Plage (Quai Gustave-Ador) is a free sand-and-grass city beach next to Parc La Grange, with showers, toilets and step-free access; reserved for children until 2007 and now open to all. Lifeguard supervision is NOT confirmed officially. Bains des Pâquis: May & Sept 10-18h, Jun-Aug 9-20h, CHF 2 adult / CHF 1 child, but it is a pebbly jetty with deeper water and stairs - better for a look and lunch than toddler paddling.

Pushchairs on Mouettes boats and trams

Solar-powered Mouettes accept unfolded pushchairs (and up to two wheelchairs); the traditional Mouettes take folded pushchairs only. Mouettes are small (roughly 15-18 people) and have no fixed timetable. Trams and buses in Geneva are largely low-floor; avoid rush hours when they are crowded. On cable cars staff may ask you to fold the buggy.

geneve.com · tripadvisor.com

Eating out: cost and kids

Geneva is expensive. Lunchtime set menus (plat du jour) run about CHF 16-30 and evening à-la-carte mains CHF 20-50; a casual main is roughly CHF 25-35. Family restaurants usually offer a children's plate for about CHF 10-14. Lunch is the cheaper meal, and many restaurants close between lunch and dinner, so plan a lunch-focused day. Kid-friendly picks named by Geneva Tourism: Chez ma Cousine, Molino, La Storia, Inglewood, Un R de Famille (brunch with play space).

help.myswitzerland.com · cagi.ch

Car seats in taxis / Uber

Swiss law: children under 12 who are under 150 cm must use a child seat in a car (CHF 60 fine). Taxis are not clearly required to supply one, so bring a travel seat or pre-book a seat-equipped transfer; the public transport card makes taxis largely unnecessary.

ch.ch · taxibambino.com

Sun, wind and cold

Even in July bring a layer: the wind off the lake 'can drop the feel by ten degrees in minutes' (LorenziGo). The Botanical Garden has unshaded stretches - bring sunscreen and water. December: the Jet d'Eau runs only 10:00-16:00 (and in 2026 it was shut 2 Nov-3 Dec for maintenance), many outdoor sites shorten hours, and Bains des Pâquis switches to sauna/fondue mode.

lorenzigo.com · ww2.sig-ge.ch

Cable cars and altitude

Toddlers are generally fine at Salève level (top station 1,100 m). Family-guide advice: stay below about 2,000 m for babies under 12 months; for higher peaks such as Jungfraujoch or Glacier 3000 forum advice is to check with a paediatrician first. Expect to fold the pushchair in cabins.

Getting there

No nonstop from Los Angeles, a seven-minute train from the airport, and from then on a city where the transport is free for anyone in a hotel.

July 2027, actually on sale

A live flight search on 8 October 2026, economy, round trip 10 to 24 July 2027, two adults and a small child:

The search returned the toddler as a third full-fare seat, so we cannot split out a child fare — treat these as the price for three seats. The cheapest premium economy was TAP at $4,945.

December 2027, not yet

Airlines open fares roughly eleven to twelve months ahead — Lufthansa about 360 days, American and Delta about 330 — so an early-December 2027 trip starts appearing around December 2026 to January 2027. As a rough guide only, the same family's December 2026 fares were $2,865 to $3,130.

Flying nonstop into Zurich and taking the train down loses on both counts in our search: three more hours on a train after an eleven-hour flight, for more money than SWISS's own connection into Geneva.

Once we land

Seven minutes

The train from the airport to Cornavin takes seven minutes. A taxi is a fixed CHF 50 to the right bank and CHF 60 to the left.

Genève Aéroport

The free ticket is gone

Plenty of older blogs still mention a free ticket from a machine in baggage reclaim. It ended on 1 January 2022; it is now CHF 3 for an hour.

airport FAQ

Then it is free anyway

Anyone staying in registered accommodation gets the Geneva Transport Card: trams, buses, trains and the lake taxis in the whole canton, including CERN and the airport train. The hotel emails it about three days before arrival.

Geneva Tourism

Under-sixes ride free

On Swiss trains, trams, buses and the lake boats, a child under six never needs a ticket.
